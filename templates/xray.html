Model details

What happens inside

  • Image decoded and checked: minimum 128 px per side, common formats, optional DICOM conversion.
  • Resized to 224×224, ImageNet normalization, single-logit sigmoid.
  • Attention map computed from the last DenseNet block with autograd.grad.
  • Quality hints (contrast, blur, aspect ratio) are warnings — never hidden rejections.
  • No score is fabricated: if inference fails, the error is shown and nothing is stored.

For research use only. Not for diagnostic use. The model was trained on pediatric X-rays from a single institution.
